About Me

I am a retired professor currently serving as a Mining Engineer in Coal India. Previously, under the World Bank-funded TEQIP-III Project, I worked as an Assistant Professor of Mining Engineering at BIT Sindri, Dhanbad.

Throughout my academic and professional journey, I have developed expertise in remote sensing, geomatics, advanced surveying techniques, mining technology, computer applications in mining, digital twin & cyber-physical systems, artificial intelligence, and robotics.

I have a deep passion for physics, algebra, and programming, as these disciplines have historically expanded our ability to model the world, enabling us to make meaningful predictions—an essential foundation for both technological progress and personal evolution.
